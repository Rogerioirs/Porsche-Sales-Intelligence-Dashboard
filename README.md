# Porsche Sales Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview

Interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed to support commercial and operational analysis of Porsche vehicle sales.

The project transforms sales data into visual indicators and interactive analyses, providing a structured view of business performance.

## Dashboard Features

- **Commercial Performance:** Revenue, sales volume and average ticket.
- **Model Analysis:** Revenue and sales quantity by vehicle model.
- **Vehicle Profile:** Analysis by model year and mileage ranges.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Sales distribution by city and state.
- **Payment Methods:** Distribution of sales by payment method.
- **Delivery Monitoring:** Analysis of vehicle delivery status.
- **Interactive Filters:** Dynamic exploration of the data.

## Technologies and Concepts

- Business Intelligence
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard
- HTML

## Project Objective

Apply Business Intelligence concepts to transform raw sales data into relevant visual information, supporting business analysis and management decision-making.

## About the Project

Developed as a practical project during a Business Intelligence learning journey, combining data organization, analytical thinking and dashboard design.
